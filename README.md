# Blockchain for Impact Funding Evidence Map – Pilot (public protocol)

This public repository holds the GSR-approved pilot protocol for the Blockchain for Impact Funding Evidence Map, led by Michael Cooper (Research Director, Metagov) for the GSR Foundation. It also holds the pilot outputs that GSR approved for public release, published at Gate 4 on 19 October 2026: the sample, collection logs, codebook, agreement reports, final coded dataset, claims register, status tracing, visuals, lessons log and the Gate 4 note.

GSR approved the pilot design (v0.1) at Gate 0.

## What is not here

Evidence packets, status-signal texts, raw coder outputs and the adjudication and audit sheets hold full third-party text, so they stay in the restricted workspace; the published files give source lists, URLs, SHA-256 hashes and quotes of 25 words or fewer instead. Partners approved publication and will fact-check their own cases while the full-scale study is prepared. Non-public partner data from the full-scale study will never be stored in this repository. Under the protocol, partner data goes into restricted storage under data-sharing agreements, and only GSR-approved, partner-reviewed, de-identified outputs are copied here.
