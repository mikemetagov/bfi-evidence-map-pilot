# Method Lessons Log (input to O16 Pilot Findings Memo)

Published 19 October 2026 at Gate 4, with GSR's approval of public release. This is a running log of method issues found during the pilot. Each entry states what happened, how the pilot handled it, the effect on pilot results, and the implication for the full-scale study. At G5 the entries feed the method-fixes and scale-up sections of the pilot findings memo (O16). Each entry links to where it is recorded in O2, O3, O18 or the protocol.

Status values:
- Open: still being observed.
- Resolved in pilot: handled by a rule.
- Carry to full scope: needs a design change or a partner data request.
