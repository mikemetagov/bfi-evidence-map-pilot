<p class="lp-eyebrow">Impact Navigator Toolkit</p>

<section class="lp-hero">
  <h1>From promising pilots to lasting impact</h1>
  <p class="lp-sub">Blockchain for good has shown what is possible. The next step is helping more projects grow beyond the pilot, using practices that other fields already rely on. The Impact Navigator Toolkit brings together two tools, the Evidence Map and the Decision Compass, to support better funding decisions at every phase.</p>
  <div class="lp-phases" role="list" aria-label="Decisions at each phase">
    <div role="listitem"><strong>Pilot</strong><span>Fund a pilot?</span></div>
    <div role="listitem"><strong>Validate</strong><span>Is it working?</span></div>
    <div role="listitem"><strong>Expand</strong><span>Continue and expand?</span></div>
    <div role="listitem"><strong>Scale</strong><span>Scale, adapt or stop?</span></div>
  </div>
  <a class="lp-btn" href="#video">Watch the introductory video</a>
</section>

<section class="lp-section">
  <h2>Building on what works</h2>
  <p class="lp-chips"><span>Health</span><span>Development</span><span>Education</span><span>Impact investing</span><span class="lp-chip-arrow" aria-hidden="true">→</span><span class="lp-chip-hi">Impact Navigator Toolkit</span></p>
  <ul class="lp-facts">
    <li>These fields already use evidence to guide funding decisions.</li>
    <li>The toolkit brings their lessons and gold-standard practices to blockchain for good.</li>
    <li>Better information at every funding gate leads to better decisions.</li>
  </ul>
</section>

<section class="lp-section">
  <h2>Two tools, one toolkit</h2>
  <div class="lp-cards lp-tools">
    <div class="lp-card">
      <h3>Evidence Map</h3><p class="lp-tag">What has been tried, and what we know</p>
      <ul>
        <li>Shows what has been tried, where, and how strong the evidence is</li>
        <li>Traces which projects continued, scaled or stopped</li>
        <li>Applies evidence-mapping methods used in health and development</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
    <div class="lp-card">
      <h3>Decision Compass</h3><p class="lp-tag">Guidance for each funding decision</p>
      <ul>
        <li>Consultative support across the whole funding lifecycle</li>
        <li>Draws on the map plus outside data, evidence and best-practice repositories</li>
        <li>Advises funders; it does not score, rank or certify</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
  </div>
  <p class="lp-band"><strong>Pilot-tested</strong> on 30 blockchain-for-good cases and 113 evidence records, and built to grow with partners.</p>
</section>

<section class="lp-section">
  <h2>The pathway beyond pilot</h2>
  <p>The Compass supports funders at each gate, with a focus on making good decisions rather than predicting outcomes.</p>
  <ol class="lp-steps">
    <li><span class="lp-num">1</span><p><strong>Design the call</strong><br>What should we fund, and what evidence counts?</p></li>
    <li><span class="lp-num">2</span><p><strong>Set thresholds</strong><br>What must a project show to be funded?</p></li>
    <li><span class="lp-num">3</span><p><strong>Mentor</strong><br>Where will support move a project furthest?</p></li>
    <li><span class="lp-num">4</span><p><strong>Decide on scale</strong><br>Continue, scale or stop, and on what basis?</p></li>
  </ol>
  <p class="lp-notice lp-foundation"><strong>Foundation: the Evidence Map and wider evidence.</strong> Every decision draws on the map, plus relevant data, evidence and best-practice repositories from international development, impact investing and other fields.</p>
</section>

<section class="lp-section">
  <h2>Managing risk, building credibility</h2>
  <dl class="lp-terms">
    <div><dt>See gaps early</dt><dd>Spot missing evidence before funds are committed.</dd></div>
    <div><dt>Decide with confidence</dt><dd>Use what comparable projects learned.</dd></div>
    <div><dt>Build credibility</dt><dd>Bring blockchain for good in line with best practice.</dd></div>
  </dl>
</section>

<section class="lp-section lp-pilot" id="pilot">
  <p class="lp-eyebrow">Evidence Map pilot</p>
  <h2>Explore the pilot</h2>
  <p>The Evidence Map collects public evidence on blockchain-for-good projects: what was tried, where, what results were reported, how strong the evidence is, and what happened after the pilot. The pilot covers 30 blockchain-for-good cases and 113 evidence records.</p>
  <a class="lp-btn" href="assistant/">Ask the Map</a>
  <p class="lp-notice"><strong>Pilot version:</strong> Ask the Map currently answers questions from the pilot evidence only, in a limited way. A full-scale query tool will follow as the Evidence Map grows.</p>
</section>

<section class="lp-section">
  <h2>How to use it</h2>
  <ol class="lp-steps">
    <li><span class="lp-num">1</span><p>Open <a href="assistant/">Ask the Map</a>.</p></li>
    <li><span class="lp-num">2</span><p>Ask a plain-language question, such as "Which projects scaled?"</p></li>
    <li><span class="lp-num">3</span><p>Read the answer and follow its citations to the evidence.</p></li>
  </ol>

<section class="lp-section">
  <h2>What's in the pilot</h2>
  <ul class="lp-facts">
    <li>30 cases and 113 evidence records, all from public sources.</li>
    <li>Each case is coded for use case, reported outcomes, strength of evidence and status after the pilot.</li>
    <li>This is a pilot: coverage is limited and will grow.</li>
  </ul>
</section>

<section class="lp-section">
  <h2>Key terms</h2>
  <dl class="lp-terms">
    <div><dt>Evidence record</dt><dd>One source describing a project's results.</dd></div>
    <div><dt>Strength of evidence</dt><dd>How much the data behind a claim can be relied on.</dd></div>
    <div><dt>Status after pilot</dt><dd>Whether a project continued, scaled or stopped.</dd></div>
    <div><dt>Pilot</dt><dd>An early, small-scale test of a project.</dd></div>
  </dl>
</section>
