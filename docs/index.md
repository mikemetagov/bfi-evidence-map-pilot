<p class="lp-eyebrow">Impact Navigator Toolkit: Evidence Map Pilot</p>

<section class="lp-hero">
  <h1>See what blockchain-for-good projects have tried, and what we know about them.</h1>
  <p class="lp-sub">A pilot Evidence Map of 30 blockchain-for-good cases and 113 evidence records, built to help funders and programme teams move projects beyond the pilot.</p>
  <a class="lp-btn" href="assistant/">Ask the Map</a>
  <p class="lp-notice"><strong>Pilot version:</strong> Ask the Map currently answers questions from the pilot evidence only, in a limited way. A full-scale query tool will follow as the Evidence Map grows.</p>
</section>

<section class="lp-section">
  <h2>What it is</h2>
  <p>The Evidence Map collects public evidence on blockchain-for-good projects: what was tried, where, what results were reported, how strong the evidence is, and what happened after the pilot. It is part of the Impact Navigator Toolkit, alongside the Decision Compass, which supports funding decisions from the first call through to scale-up.</p>
</section>

<section class="lp-section">
  <h2>How to use it</h2>
  <ol class="lp-steps">
    <li><span class="lp-num">1</span><p>Open <a href="assistant/">Ask the Map</a>.</p></li>
    <li><span class="lp-num">2</span><p>Ask a plain-language question, such as "Which projects scaled?"</p></li>
    <li><span class="lp-num">3</span><p>Read the answer and follow its citations to the evidence.</p></li>
  </ol>
</section>

<section class="lp-section">
  <h2>What's in the pilot</h2>
  <ul class="lp-facts">
    <li>30 cases and 113 evidence records, all from public sources.</li>
    <li>Each case is coded for use case, reported outcomes, strength of evidence and status after the pilot.</li>
    <li>This is a pilot: coverage is limited and will grow.</li>
  </ul>
</section>

<section class="lp-section">
  <h2>Key terms</h2>
  <dl class="lp-terms">
    <div><dt>Evidence record</dt><dd>One source describing a project's results.</dd></div>
    <div><dt>Strength of evidence</dt><dd>How much the data behind a claim can be relied on.</dd></div>
    <div><dt>Status after pilot</dt><dd>Whether a project continued, scaled or stopped.</dd></div>
    <div><dt>Pilot</dt><dd>An early, small-scale test of a project.</dd></div>
  </dl>
</section>
