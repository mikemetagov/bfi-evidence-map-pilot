# Explore the evidence map

Rows are the six use-case domains; columns are the six outcome domains (A–F). Each bubble is one evidence level, sized by the number of use cases. Hover over or tab to a bubble to preview its cases, click to pin the card and follow the source links, and press Esc to close.

!!! info "Pilot data, published at Gate 4 (19 October 2026)"
    30 pilot cases from four portfolios; a purposive sample, not representative of all funded use cases. Quotes are cut to 25 words; full-text hashes are in the [O12 outcomes table](../outputs/09_Final_Dataset/O12_outcomes_v1.0.csv). Partners will fact-check their own cases while the full-scale study is prepared.

!!! warning "Filter gaps"
    Where the two coders differed on a non-core field, the field is left blank for the pilot and will be fixed in codebook v1.3. 21 of 30 cases have at least one blank filter field (37 blanks): country 5, region 4, year launched 9, blockchain functions 12, implementation model 7. Filtering on one of these fields leaves those cases out. The coder values are in the [non-core differences table](../outputs/09_Final_Dataset/O12_noncore_differences_v1.0.csv).

!!! note "Reading the map"
    A use case appears once for each outcome domain it was coded on, so totals across cells can exceed the number of use cases. Hatched cells have no coded evidence. Gap types (absolute, outcome, counterfactual, added-value and harms gaps) are defined in section 9.1 of the protocol.
