# Explore the evidence map

Rows are the six use-case domains; columns are the six outcome domains (A–F). Each bubble is one evidence level, sized by the number of use cases. Hover over or tab to a bubble to preview its cases, click to pin the card and follow the source links, and press Esc to close.

!!! info "Not yet published"
    The map is empty by design. Coded pilot data stays restricted until GSR approves its release; full-scale data is published only after partner review under the data-sharing agreements.

!!! note "Reading the map"
    A use case appears once for each outcome domain it was coded on, so totals across cells can exceed the number of use cases. Hatched cells have no coded evidence. Gap types (absolute, outcome, counterfactual, added-value and harms gaps) are defined in section 9.1 of the protocol.
